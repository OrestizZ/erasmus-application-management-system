What is the Erasmus+ Program?

Erasmus+ is one of the largest student mobility programs in Europe. It gives university students the opportunity to spend a semester or a full academic year at a partner institution abroad, improving their language skills and gaining valuable academic and cultural experiences.

Partner Universities

  • University of Peloponnese - A historic university with EXCEPTIONAL human resources.
  • University of Crete - One of the oldest universities in Greece, renowned for its language programs.
  • University of West Attica - Specializes in technological and medical sciences.
